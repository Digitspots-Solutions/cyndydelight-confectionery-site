About Us

CyndyDelight Confectionery is a proud Nigerian confectionery manufacturer based in the heart of Owerri, Imo State. Located at 32A Orlu Road, Amakohia Uratta, we have built our reputation on delivering exceptional quality baked goods and confectioneries that make every occasion memorable.

Our state-of-the-art manufacturing facility combines traditional baking expertise with modern techniques to create products that delight the senses. From birthday cakes and wedding cakes to small chops, pastries, and everyday treats, we craft each item with premium ingredients and meticulous attention to detail.

Serving Owerri North and surrounding communities, we're committed to making your celebrations sweeter and your everyday moments more special. Our team of skilled bakers and confectioners work tirelessly to ensure every product that leaves our facility meets the highest standards of taste, freshness, and presentation.
