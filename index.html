Distinct Pharma Pay Hub Nigeria is a pioneering fintech company specializing in embedded finance solutions for the pharmaceutical and healthcare sector. Founded by an experienced Embedded Finance Architect, we understand the unique financial challenges faced by pharmacies, drug distributors, and healthcare providers across Nigeria. Our mission is to bridge the gap between traditional pharmaceutical commerce and modern digital banking, providing tailored payment infrastructure that ensures security, compliance, and operational efficiency. Based in Owerri, Imo State, we serve businesses throughout Nigeria with cutting-edge technology that simplifies transactions, improves cash flow management, and enhances customer experience. Frequently Asked Questions

What types of businesses can use Distinct Pharma Pay Hub?

We serve community pharmacies, hospital pharmacies, pharmaceutical distributors, medical supply stores, and healthcare facilities of all sizes across Nigeria.

How long does setup take?

Most businesses are fully operational within 48-72 hours. Complex integrations with multiple locations may take up to one week, with our technical team managing the entire process.

What are your transaction fees?

Our pricing is transparent and competitive, typically ranging from 0.5% to 1.5% depending on transaction volume and payment method. We provide custom quotes based on your specific business needs with no hidden charges.

Is my customer data secure?

Absolutely. We employ bank-level 256-bit encryption, are PCI-DSS compliant, and follow all Central Bank of Nigeria data protection guidelines. Your customer information is never shared with third parties.

Can I integrate with my existing pharmacy software?

Yes. We have pre-built integrations with major pharmacy management systems used in Nigeria and can develop custom integrations for specialized software.

What payment methods do you support?

We support all major payment methods: Verve, Mastercard, Visa cards; bank transfers; USSD codes; mobile money (including Paga, OPay); QR codes; and cash collection with digital receipts.

Do you provide hardware like POS terminals?

Yes. We supply modern POS terminals optimized for pharmacy operations, including receipt printers and barcode scanners. Hardware costs are separate from transaction fees.

What kind of support do you offer?

We provide 24/7 technical support via phone, WhatsApp, and email. Our Owerri-based team
